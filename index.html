Origin to port

Three steps from farm to your warehouse

1

Source and sample

Tell us your crop, grade and volume. We match you with partner cooperatives and send samples for cupping or lab testing.

2

Inspect and contract

Agree price and terms (FOB, LC or TT). Quality and quantity are verified before loading.

3

Ship and deliver

Goods move to Djibouti Port or by air cargo with phytosanitary certificate, origin certificate and bill of lading.

About us

Empowering Ethiopian agriculture through direct global market access

ET Crop Chain links Ethiopian growers and exporters of coffee, oilseeds, pulses, fruit, vegetables and herbs with international buyers. We make trading simple, transparent and dependable for both sides.

Our mission

Give Ethiopian producers a direct, fair route to global markets, and give buyers reliable, well-documented supply.

Local expertise

Based in Addis Ababa, with knowledge of Ethiopia’s growing regions and the Ethiopian Commodity Exchange (ECX).

Partner network

Grower cooperatives, storage facilities and logistics partners working together from farm to port.
